Difference

A unit of the text that changed between the two versions of a comparison. Its JSON Schema is difference.schema.json. If the comparison has no difference at the unit, the status is 404 Not Found.

Members

eId
The eId of the unit. For example, art_3 or anx/pnt_1.2.
kind
inserted: only the later version has the unit. deleted: only the earlier version has the unit. changed: the wording of the unit changed.
unitKind
What the unit is. For example, article.
num
The number of the unit as printed. For example, (a). Empty if it has none.
inserted, deleted
How many words the change inserted and deleted.
parent
The eId of the unit that holds the unit. Null for an outermost unit.
section
The eId of the unit with a title that holds the unit, as the outline has them.
markers, earlierMarkers
The amendment markers of the later text and of the earlier text: which acts gave the unit its form, and how.
amendments
The changes that the Publications Office records at the unit between the two versions, as in Amendments.
before, after
The plain text of the unit before and after the change.
changes
The runs of words, in order. op is equal for words in both versions, insert for words only in the later version, and delete for words only in the earlier version. text is the words. A line feed ends each paragraph, and each number and heading of a unit in the unit, except the last.
shown
The unit, as a block of the text. In its HTML, ins and del are around the words that changed. Its plain text is the text after the change.
