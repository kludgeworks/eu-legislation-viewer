Difference

An occurrence of the text that changed between the two versions of a comparison. Its JSON Schema is difference.schema.json. If the comparison has no difference at the occurrence, the status is 404 Not Found.

Members

eId
The eId of the occurrence. For example, art_3 or anx/pnt_1.2.
kind
inserted: only the later version has the occurrence. deleted: only the earlier version has the occurrence. changed: the wording of the occurrence changed.
occurrenceKind
What the occurrence is. For example, article.
num
The number of the occurrence as printed. For example, (a). Empty if it has none.
inserted, deleted
How many words the change inserted and deleted.
parent
The eId of the occurrence that holds the occurrence. Null for an outermost occurrence.
section
The eId of the occurrence with a title that holds the occurrence, as the outline has them.
tags, earlierTags
The tags of the later text and of the earlier text: which acts gave the occurrence its form, and how.
amendments
The changes that the Publications Office records at the occurrence between the two versions, as in Amendments.
before, after
The plain text of the occurrence before and after the change.
changes
The runs of words, in order. op is equal for words in both versions, insert for words only in the later version, and delete for words only in the earlier version. text is the words. A line feed ends each paragraph, and each number and heading of an occurrence in the occurrence, except the last.
shown
The occurrence, as a block of the text. In its HTML, ins and del are around the words that changed. Its plain text is the text after the change.
