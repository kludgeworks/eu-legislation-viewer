Preparation

The preparation of the texts of an act in one language. The viewer converts the text of each version in that language, from the Formex of the Publications Office. See Waiting.

Members

language
The language, by the EU's code. For example, eng.
started
When someone first asked for the act in the language.
settledAt
When its last text was made or failed. Null while the viewer prepares it.
ready
How many texts are ready.
failed
How many texts failed.
converting
How many texts the viewer makes now.
ahead
About how many texts of all acts are before its texts in the queue, while the viewer makes none of its texts.

The progress of each text is in _embedded.item. A text that is ready links to it, av:text. Each has:

version
Where the version is among the versions: oj, or its date.
celex
The CELEX number of the document that holds the text.
progress
waiting, converting, ready or failed.
attempt
The attempt that runs, or the last attempt that failed while the text waits. 0 before the first attempt and when the text is ready or failed.
failure
Why the text failed, as in the problem text-failed. Null unless it failed.
retryable
Whether to try again may help. Null unless it failed.

Links

up
The act.
av:events

The changes of the preparation, as a stream of server-sent events (text/event-stream). Use it to wait for a text without asking again.

preparation
The preparation changed. The data is its progress, as JSON.
settled
Every text is ready or failed. The data is the progress at the end. Then the stream ends.

The data has no links. To follow a link, ask for the preparation.

av:retry
Tries again to make the texts that failed, if to try again may help. Send POST, with no body. The answer is 202 Accepted, with the preparation. The link is there only while such a text exists.
