A unit of the text that changed between the two versions. The link is a template with one variable, eId. For example, art_3 or anx/pnt_1.2.

Its kind tells how the unit changed:

inserted
Only the later version has the unit.
deleted
Only the earlier version has the unit.
changed
The wording of the unit changed.

A difference also has the number of words inserted and deleted, the amendment markers of the consolidation, and the acts that amended the unit. In shown, it has the unit as a block of the text. In the HTML, ins and del are around the words that changed. The plain text is the text after the change.
